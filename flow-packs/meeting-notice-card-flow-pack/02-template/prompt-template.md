# 會議通知小卡提示詞模板

```text
Create a clean meeting notice card for an elementary school administrative context.
Use a warm, formal, readable visual style.
Keep text large, sparse, and readable on a phone.
Do not generate logos, seals, stamps, watermarks, QR codes, meeting links, phone numbers, or personal data.
```

## 會議通知小卡

```text
Generate one 16:9 horizontal meeting notice card image.

If the publishing channel is not decided yet, start with a 16:9 horizontal draft.
If the card is mainly for LINE, it can be revised into a vertical card after the text is confirmed.
If the card is mainly for email, slides, or a web page, keep it horizontal.

Create a warm, clean, official-but-friendly elementary school administrative notice card.

Main visible text:
頂溪國小 會議通知
時間：____ 年 ____ 月 ____ 日（星期__）____:____
地點：__________
主題：__________
提醒：請準時出席，並攜帶相關資料

Visual direction:
- Traditional Chinese only.
- Text must be large and readable on a phone.
- Use warm ivory, deep green, soft gold, and subtle administrative blue.
- Include simple meeting-related visuals such as calendar, clipboard, folder, pen, meeting table, or checklist.
- Do not generate logos, seals, stamps, watermarks, QR codes, meeting links, phone numbers, or page numbers.
- Do not include fake names, real personal data, meeting IDs, QR codes, or contact information.
```
