# 品牌圖像提示詞模板

```text
Create a clean administrative announcement image for an elementary school.
Use a warm, formal, readable visual style.
Leave the top-right corner empty for an official crest overlay.
Do not generate any school crest, logo, seal, stamp, watermark, or official emblem.
Keep text large, sparse, and readable.
Do not place important text or faces near the edges.
```

## 會議通知小卡

```text
Generate one 16:9 horizontal meeting notice card image.

Create a warm, clean, official-but-friendly elementary school administrative notice card.

Main visible text:
頂溪國小 會議通知
時間：____ 年 ____ 月 ____ 日（星期__）____:____
地點：__________
主題：__________
提醒：請準時出席，並攜帶相關資料

Visual direction:
- Traditional Chinese only.
- Text must be large and readable on a phone.
- Use warm ivory, deep green, soft gold, and subtle administrative blue.
- Include simple meeting-related visuals such as calendar, clipboard, folder, pen, meeting table, or checklist.
- Do not generate any school crest, logo, seal, stamp, official emblem, watermark, or page number.
- Leave the top-right corner clean and empty for a school crest overlay if needed.
- Do not include fake names, real personal data, meeting IDs, QR codes, or contact information.
```
